# Credits

Every picture in this folder is a photograph somebody else took, found with
`.claude/skills/photofind` and kept under the licence it was published with.

Seven are CC0 or public domain and owe nothing. Two are CC BY and owe a
credit, which is this file. Three are CC BY-SA, and that one is not just a
credit: a cutout is a derivative, so those three carry share-alike, and so
does anything that redistributes them. It is written down here rather than
remembered.

Each `.webp` was cut out of its photograph with Vision's foreground mask and
scaled so its longest side fills the same box as the others. Nothing was
added and nothing was painted out. `playing-cards` is a crop of its source,
taken to leave out the hand that was placing the deck.

These are real products, photographed by their owners or by passers-by, and
the labels on them are real makers' labels. That is what a duty-free trolley
carries, so it is what the pictures show — but it does mean this cabin is
displaying other people's marks, and a shipping aircraft would need their
say-so rather than only the photographer's.
